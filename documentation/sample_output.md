## LOGICAL GAPS

1. The memo assumes that “rigorous imprisonment for two years or upwards” in § 120B(2) includes any term of three years, even if the sentence is not exclusively rigorous.  
   - The excerpt is silent on whether the phrase covers offences punishable with “simple” as well as “rigorous” imprisonment.

2. The discussion presupposes that § 120B(2) automatically imports the exact abetment punishment without textual incorporation of §§ 107–116 requirements.  
   - The supplied text only says “punished in the same manner as if he had abetted,” leaving open whether *mens rea* or *actus reus* elements of abetment must also be proved.

3. The memo does not address whether conspiracy under § 120A is an offence of mere agreement or demands an overt act; it correctly notes the data gap but stops short of signalling that this could be outcome-determinative.

---

## SUGGESTED CORRECTIONS

- Add a footnote highlighting the interpretive ambiguity whether “rigorous imprisonment for two years or upwards” is satisfied when the offence is punishable with either simple or rigorous imprisonment.  
- Clarify that the phrase “as if he had abetted” is unresolved on whether it imports only the penalty quantum or also the substantive elements of abetment.  
- Recommend obtaining the full scheme of §§ 107–116 IPC in addition to § 120A to test the prosecution’s evidentiary burden.
